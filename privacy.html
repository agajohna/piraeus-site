PIRAEUS

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

Piraeus does not collect, store, or share your personal data. Everything it does happens on your Mac.

How Piraeus reads comments

On YouTube and X, Piraeus reads the comments on the page you are viewing and decides, on your Mac, whether to hide each one. Comment text goes from Safari to the Piraeus extension on the same Mac and nowhere else. Results are kept only in memory while the page is open, and are discarded when you leave it.

What Piraeus saves

Piraeus saves only your settings: which mode you chose (Ascend, Protect My Peace, or Descend). They are kept in Safari's local extension storage on your Mac and are removed when you uninstall Piraeus.

What Piraeus never does

  • It never sends comments, pages you visit, or anything else over the internet. The extension has no network access.
  • It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • It does not run on websites other than YouTube and X.

Safari permissions

Safari asks you to allow Piraeus on youtube.com and x.com. That access is used only to read and hide comments on those sites, as described above. You can change it at any time in Safari Settings → Extensions.

Children

Piraeus collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
