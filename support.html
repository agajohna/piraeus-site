Getting started

  • Open the Piraeus app once, then turn on Piraeus in Safari Settings → Extensions.
  • Allow it on YouTube and X when Safari asks, or in the same settings pane.
  • Click the Piraeus chevron in the Safari toolbar and choose a mode.

The modes

  • Ascend hides comments that attack people: insults, threats, slurs, and contemptuous sneers. Disagreement and criticism stay.
  • Protect My Peace is stricter. It also hides sneering, mocking, and media replies on X, while keeping clearly kind or thoughtful comments.
  • Descend shows everything.

Common questions

Nothing is being hidden. Check that Piraeus is turned on in Safari Settings → Extensions and allowed on the site, and that the toolbar mode isn't Descend. Reload the page after changing settings.

A fair comment was hidden, or a cruel one was shown. Piraeus decides on your Mac with an on-device model and will sometimes get it wrong. Switch to Descend to see everything on a page. Examples sent by email help improve future versions.

Does Piraeus work on other sites? Not yet. It works on YouTube and X in Safari on the Mac.

Does Piraeus send my data anywhere? No. See the privacy policy.
